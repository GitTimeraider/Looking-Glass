Snooze & maintenance

For a while, alerts that match are stored silently: not shown as open and not mailed - but they still count for attack chains and the risk score, so a real attack during the maintenance is not lost. Alerts that were already open stay open, but are not mailed while the snooze lasts. For something that should stay quiet for good, use Mute similar… on the Alerts page instead. Something that happens at the same time every week (a backup on Tuesday night): Settings › Alert filters › Recurring mute windows.
